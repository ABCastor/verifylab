# Research map

Link the problem, established results and their limits, useful negative evidence, uncertain routes,
missing bridges and promising next investigations. Explain why a route helps the objective.

Link exact item revisions and artifacts. Read current proof status with `vl show`; do not copy it as
an enduring fact. Preserve important objections and disagreement when distilling the map.

This is a selectable context source. Researchers can use rich, sparse or fresh context and choose
their own route, including alternatives and challenges that may themselves solve the problem.
