# Purpose of the toy proof

**Who.** A learner and their assistant.

**Purpose.** Make the route from a supplied certificate to the conclusion easy to read.

**Source.** The bundled Tiny project, in `fixtures/foreign-lake`.

**Contract.** Keep the statement of `Tiny.conclusion` and its toolchain fixed. Rebuild before claiming acceptance.

**Values and resources.** Track changed source lines and minutes. Price each candidate before editing.
The learner judges whether the new route is easier to follow.

Check the two open forecasts from the VerifyLab checkout with
`vl attempts check examples/toy-attempts/attempts`.
